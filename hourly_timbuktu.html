7 Day Hourly Temperature

What is this?

This is a way to more intuitively view and understand the temperatures in your area in the upcoming days. Each square represents the temperature of each hour for the next week, starting with the upcoming hour. The color of each block depends on which temperature range it falls into, in 10 degree increments. You can hover over a square to get the specific temperature for that hour. This can help you to understand, at a glance, how the temperature will behave throughout the upcoming days, allowing you to plan your outfit accordingly. If you see blocks of cooler temperatures, that means in those hours it will be cooler, and you might want to plan to wear warmer clothes during that time period. Likewise, if you see blocks of warmer colors, it means those hours will be comparatively warmer compared to the rest of the day, and you might want to make sure you wear clothes that will allow you to be comfortable when the temperature rises. This can be especially useful when planning a vacation, as you can get a sense for how the weather will be at your destination during the duration of your stay, allowing you to pack appropriate clothing. The legend at the bottom shows which colors correspond to which ranges of temperatures. The temperature information is acquired from the (Weather Underground API).
