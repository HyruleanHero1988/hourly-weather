    This is a 16-day hourly weather dashboard. Temperature, precipitation chance, and cloud cover, each shown as a color-coded grid.

    A heat wave looks like a warm-colored bubble. A cold snap looks like a cool-colored one. Rainy trends and overcast patterns show up the same way. The patterns jump out at a glance; no more scanning columns of numbers.

    Click any square for hourly details. Use the gear icon to switch between temperature, precipitation, and cloud cover. Useful for travel, packing, or just understanding the week ahead.

    Weather data from Open-Meteo.
